# Mains parts: source evidence (Task 0)

Date: 2026-09-27. Branch `mains`. Research only; no part is generated by this task.

The machine-readable table is `src/format/mainsEvidence.ts`: per module id, the sources, every value with the line it was read from, and a verdict. This log explains how the values were read, what was rejected, and every item that needs Michael's decision before Tasks 16 to 20 generate anything.

## How the sources were read

- Datasheets were downloaded and read as text (`pdftotext`) and, where the value sits in a drawing or a table image, rendered and looked at (Hi-Link pinout, Songle rating tables, Fotek specification and terminal photo, WAGO rating tables, KCD1 drawing, NEMA WD 6 figures, JIS C 8303 Figure A.1).
- Manufacturer web pages that render with scripts (Leviton, Phoenix Contact, Philips, Littelfuse, LCSC, Kefa) were opened in a headless Chrome launched through `playwright-core` (not the shared Playwright MCP browser) and their text saved.
- Every quote from a web summary was checked against the raw page text; two summaries turned out to be wrong and were not used (a "10/16 A up to 250 V" figure that Wikipedia gives for CEE 7/1, not CEE 7/5; and a WAGO summary that omitted the ratings).
- Isolation: only a named insulation class counts. No source for any converter, relay or SSR in this list names one, so no entry carries `isolation`. The test voltages each source gives are kept in `extra.isolationNote` or `extra.dielectric`.
- Services: NEMA, JIS and the plug and socket figures are AC wiring device ratings (`ac`). IEC 60664 rated voltages (Phoenix, WAGO) and the clone listings do not say AC or DC, so they are `ac/dc`. The plan's parts tests (Tasks 18 and 19) expect `service: 'ac'` on the WAGO and Phoenix modules; the generators should take the service from the evidence, and those test lines should follow it.

## Blocking items for Michael

**B1. Paywalled standards (outlets and plugs for UK, EU, FR, AU).** BS 1363, AS/NZS 3112, CEE 7 (and DIN 49440, NF C 61-314, EN 50075) and IEC/TR 60083 are sold, and no public copy was found (archive.org, public.resource.org, national sites). What is recorded comes from Wikipedia (BS 1363, AS/NZS 3112, Schuko, Europlug, AC power plugs and sockets) and, for AS/NZS 3112, a supplier guide (Access Communications); the two AS sources agree. Where no source gave a rated voltage (CEE 7/3, CEE 7/5, CEE 7/7), none is recorded: the plan's 16 A 250 V figures are unsourced. Proposed: either accept these secondary sources for layout and L side (and pick a real product per socket for the rating: MK, Gira, Legrand, Clipsal datasheets), or buy IEC/TR 60083. Until then these parts are not generated.

**B2. No insulation class for the Hi-Link modules, the relay module or the Fotek SSR (Resolution 21).**
- Hi-Link HLK-PM01/PM03: the product pages say "Input and output isolation voltage 3000VAC"; datasheet V2.6 (9.2) says "Insulation voltage I/P-O/P:2500Vac" (the two disagree) and "Safety standard meets UL1012,EN60950,UL60950". No class. Also: "The UL and CE certifications are made by the customer".
- Songle SRD-05VDC-SL-C: "Dielectric strength ... Between coil and contacts 1500VAC 1min". "Insulation level B/F" is the coil's thermal class, not a coil-to-contact insulation class. The module listings state nothing ("The Maximum voltage that can pass through the Switched (NO/NC) side of the relays is written on them").
- Fotek SSR-25DA: "Isolation strength 4 KVrms (EN60950/VDE0805)", "Insulation strength 100MΩ / 500VDC". No class.

With `isolation: "unknown"` the checker treats the low-voltage side as live when the mains side is energized. Consequences: the spec circuit "HLK-PM01 feeding an ESP32 (clean apart from cable-unverified)" becomes a `mains-to-low-voltage` error on the ESP32 pins, and "relay switching a fused lamp from a US outlet (only the relay module's unverified-rating and cable-unverified warnings)" becomes an error on the relay's IN, DC- and DC+ wiring; an SSR driven from a GPIO does too. **The relay module and HLK circuits cannot be clean as written.** Options for Michael: (a) keep `unknown` and change those expected results to the errors (most honest, but every such hobby circuit lights up red); (b) allow an explicit maker test voltage at or above a threshold to count as `basic` only (still not SELV; the circuits would then report inadequate separation); (c) look for a different reference part whose datasheet names reinforced insulation (for example a Mean Well IRM-03-5 or a RECOM RAC03 module, both of which are sold with SELV outputs and whose datasheets may state the class; not researched here) and keep HLK as `unknown`.

**B3. No insulation class for the plug-in chargers and adapters.** CUI (the plan's first choice) has no UK or AU version of its SWI USB or barrel adapters, and states only "isolation voltage input to output ... 3,000 Vac". Mean Well NGE12 was chosen because one series covers all four regions with interchangeable plugs; it states "Class II power (no earth pin)", "WITHSTAND VOLTAGE I/P-O/P:4000Vac" and shows a "2xMOPP" icon, but no class for the barrier. Under the rule "a class II symbol ... alone is not a class" the outputs are `unknown`, so every charger or adapter output would be treated as live. Proposed: Michael rules whether a maker's written "Class II" statement (not just a symbol) may be recorded as `double`; IEC 61140 class II equipment is defined by double or reinforced insulation, but this is exactly the inference the plan forbids without his decision.

**B4. AS/NZS 3112: plan Task 12 has L on the wrong side.** Both sources put the active at the top left seen from the front with the earth pin down ("The active terminal is the first 'socket' from the earth 'socket' in a clockwise direction when viewing the front of a socket-outlet"; "On the socket (viewing from the front) ... top-left is Active and top-right is Neutral"). Task 12's AS pattern has L at (10, -10), top right. Task 12 must swap L and N for `as3112` (pattern and plug profile together).

**B5. CEE 7/5 (French): L side not fixed.** Task 12 puts L on the left for all CEE patterns and Resolution 22 treats `cee7-5` as polarized. No source read fixes the L side of a French socket: "Niveau norme, il n'y a rien qui indique que la phase doit être branchée à droite ou à gauche", with usage putting the phase on the right, seen from the front. The CEE 7/7 plug can only enter one way, but the socket's own wiring is not standardized. Proposed: add `cee7-5` to Resolution 22's unpolarized list (polarity findings become "may"), keep the 0-only orientation rule.

**B6. UK plug fuse.** Mean Well does not state whether AC PLUG-UK4 carries a BS 1362 fuse or its rating. The UK charger and adapter cannot have a sourced integral fuse edge. Proposed: generate the UK devices without a fuse rating (fuse present, rating unknown: `fuse-rating-unknown`) only if Michael accepts that a UK plug-in device carries a BS 1362 fuse (BS 1363 plugs are fused, per the secondary sources of B1; the UK Plugs and Sockets etc. (Safety) Regulations 1994 were not read); otherwise not generated.

**B7. E27 shell on N.** BS 7671 reg. 559.5.1.206 (as discussed on the IET forum; the regulation text itself was not read) requires the outer contact of Edison screw lampholders on N but exempts E14 and E27 lampholders to BS EN 60238. No free source for the IEC 60364 equivalent was found. The E26 requirement is sourced (29 CFR 1910.305(j)(1): "the grounded conductor shall be connected to the screw shell"). Proposed: E27 holder without an N requirement on the shell (no polarity finding), unless Michael wants it kept as good practice with "may" wording.

**B8. Phoenix Contact item numbers for 3 to 6 positions.** phoenixcontact.com answered HTTP 403 (bot protection) after the first few product pages. Read so far: 1757019, 1757242, 1757255, 1803578, 1803277, 1803581 (see `PHOENIX_READ` in the evidence file for any read later). The unread item numbers are the plan's and are recorded as such; those parts are not generated until each page is read (a retry in a day, or Michael pasting the pages).

## Other findings and contradictions with the plan or spec

- **MC 1,5 on 230 V.** Phoenix rates MC 1,5 plug and header at 160 V for overvoltage category III (pollution degree 3 and 2); only under II/2 does the assembly reach 250 V (the header's II/2 value; the plug's is 320 V). On a 230 V sheet an MC 1,5 is adequate only under the II/2 condition, so it should always carry `rating-conditional`.
- **Phoenix plug and header differ.** MSTB 2,5 plug III/3 is 250 V but the MSTBA header is 320 V; MC 1,5 plug II/2 is 320 V but the header is 250 V. The evidence keeps the lower of each pair per condition.
- **Phoenix: no hot plugging.** "COMBICON connectors have no switching power (COC) ... must not be plugged in or disconnected when carrying voltage or under load." Worth a note in the part.
- **Fuse holder reference.** The plan's Schurter FPG4 is a PCB-mount vertical holder ("Mounting PCB, Terminal Solder THT"), not an in-line holder. Littelfuse 150274 (01500274Z) is an in-line 5 x 20 mm holder: "10 amperes at 350V for the 5 × 20mm size fuses", AC and DC 350 V. Littelfuse adds "If use above 32V, power must be turn off when changing the fuse."
- **Lamp holder currents.** Leviton 9880 gives 250 V and 660 W, no current; the E26 rating therefore has no `amps`.
- **Vossloh-Schwabe "4/250".** The catalogue writes the rating as "nominal rating: 4/250" without units; 4 A 250 V is the IEC 60238 marking convention. Recorded as 4 A 250 V with that note.
- **Hi-Link input range.** Product pages: 85-264 VAC; datasheet V2.6: 85-265 Vac; the narrower is kept. Rated input is 100-240 Vac on both.
- **Hi-Link datasheet source.** hlktech.com links the PDF only through Google Drive; the V2.6 copy read is a mirror (geeksvalley.com). The product pages (hlktech.net) are Hi-Link's own.
- **Songle contact ratings.** The current Songle SRD (T73) sheet gives "Contact rating(Res. load) 7A/10A/15A 250VAC/28VDC, 10A 125VAC" without splitting by contact form; the older sheet gives FORM C: 7 A 28 VDC, 10 A 125 VAC, 7 A 240 VAC (resistive). The older, form-specific figures are the ratings; both are recorded.
- **Fotek current revision.** The series page and the catalogue (PDF created 2022-06-21) give "Input voltage 4 ~ 32 VDC" (the "3-32 VDC" on many listings is not Fotek's current figure), "Turn off voltage <3.5 VDC", "Trigger current 12.0mA max.", "Leakage current 5 mA max.", "Output voltage 24 ~ 380VAC", "Rated current 25A max." for a resistive load. Derating is given as notes, not a curve: thermal grease on a heatsink is required; for incandescent lamps "the rated current of the module must be over 4 times of the incandescent lamp current"; the Fotek HS-50 heatsink is rated "15A max." per SSR. Terminal layout from Fotek's own product photo: top 1 (left) and 2 (right), bottom 4 (-, left) and 3 (+, right). SSR-25DA is widely counterfeited.
- **JIS C 8303 and unpolarized receptacles.** JIS C 8303:2007 note b) allows unpolarized devices "except receptacles" (コンセントを除き), so a new JIS receptacle is polarized; Figure A.1 note a) still defines the unpolarized dimensions. The unpolarized outlet is the older one still in service.
- **NEMA letter W.** WD 6 marks W and G on the faces but the pages read do not define W; the L side rests on the drawing (W is the longer slot) plus Wikipedia's explicit statement. This is the only place a US value leans on a secondary source.
- **Resolution 7 and NEMA 5-15P.** WD 6 shows two 5-15P variants: "ONE WIDE BLADE" and "NARROW BLADES"; either seats in a 5-15R only one way because of the ground pin.
